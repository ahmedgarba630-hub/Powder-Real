FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Straight answers on integration, licensing, automation and security.

Powder is infrastructure, not an off-the-shelf app — a set of APIs and services that handle quoting, issuance, collection, claims and administration, so you can build insurance experiences into your own product without becoming a licensed insurer yourself.

Most teams integrate the core quote-and-issue flow within a couple of weeks using our sandbox and SDKs, with full production rollout typically following shortly after compliance review.

We support embedded cover across device protection, health, gig-worker insurance, and checkout-based protection products, with more verticals opening up as licensing partners expand.

All policies are underwritten by licensed insurance partners, and Powder's infrastructure is built to align with regional data protection and insurance regulatory requirements.

It means the core quoting, issuance and claims APIs are engineered for enterprise-grade uptime, with monitoring and redundancy so your integration keeps working even during peak transaction volumes.

A small integration effort from your existing engineers is usually enough — our SDKs and docs are built so most teams don't need to hire specifically for the integration.
