Powder is an embedded insurance platform that enables businesses to offer insurance at the point of transaction. Whether customers are checking out online, paying for healthcare, booking travel, or applying for a loan, Powder makes it easy to embed relevant insurance into those experiences.

Powder is designed for businesses that want to offer insurance without becoming insurance companies. We work with fintechs, logistics companies, healthcare providers, e-commerce platforms, mobility companies, banks, telecoms, and other digital businesses.

Powder connects your business to licensed insurance providers through a single integration. When a customer reaches a relevant point in their journey, your platform can present an insurance offer, issue a policy instantly, collect premiums, and manage claims — all without leaving your product.

No. Businesses integrate with Powder while insurance products are underwritten by licensed insurance partners. This allows you to offer protection without managing the regulatory complexity of being an insurer.

Depending on your business model, you can offer products such as device protection, goods-in-transit insurance, health insurance, travel insurance, motor insurance, loan protection, gig worker insurance, and other embedded insurance products through the Powder platform.

Most businesses can begin integrating within days using our APIs and developer tools. Our team works closely with you to configure products, connect insurance partners, and prepare for launch.

Yes. Powder is API-first and designed to integrate with existing applications, checkout systems, payment platforms, CRMs, ERPs, and digital products with minimal disruption.
